## Q&A Prep

### If Asked: Is This A Chatbot?

> No. The chat-style screen is just the intake interface. The product is a workflow: classify the enquiry, retrieve evidence, decide answerability, draft only when supported, route gaps, draft FAQ updates, and generate marketing intelligence.

### If Asked: Does Telemetry Retrain The Model?

> Not automatically. Telemetry captures reviewer actions, useful evidence, edits, rejected claims, and resolved gaps. That improves the workflow through better KB coverage, retrieval tuning, routing rules, and evaluation data. Automatic model retraining would be a later controlled step, not something happening blindly in production.

### If Asked: What Makes It Safe?

> Three things: an evidence gate before drafting, human approval before customer delivery, and a gap workflow when evidence is missing. The scorecard shows zero unsupported hard-claim guardrail failures in the current evaluation.

### If Asked: What Data Is Real?

> The system uses the six actual local BOLDR files: customer tickets, FAQ PDF, SOP, product reference, engraving rate card, and servicing rate card. It does not invent missing files from the brief.

### If Asked: What Is The ROI?

> The pilot cost is roughly 37 to 75 dollars per month before credits. The value comes from faster drafting, less repeated source research, fewer risky claims, reusable FAQ drafts, and product-page or campaign signals from repeated customer questions.

### If Asked About `~24 hrs/wk`

> I would treat that as a pilot estimate, not a production guarantee. It assumes a small support team avoids repeated manual lookup and repeated gap-resolution work across recurring enquiries. In production, I would validate it by measuring baseline handle time against SignalDesk-assisted handle time.
