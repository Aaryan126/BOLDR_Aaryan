# BOLDR Revenue Rocket Video Script

Target runtime: 3:30 maximum.

| Time | Show on screen | Exact narration | Rubric point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:25 | Customer Chat landing screen and source metrics | "BOLDR is a small watch e-commerce brand with a support problem. Customers ask about straps, engraving, shipping, servicing, materials, and sustainability. A small CS team can answer tickets, but the intelligence gets lost: repeated questions, missing FAQ updates, and product-page gaps. Revenue Rocket turns each enquiry into a support answer, a knowledge gap, or a marketing signal." | Real SME problem |
| 0:25-0:55 | System Details or workflow trace | "The workflow is evidence-first. It ingests the enquiry, classifies intent and one required buyer persona, searches the local BOLDR knowledge base, checks answerability, and only then drafts. Rate cards win for pricing and turnaround. Unsupported claims, order lookups, and uncertain facts are routed to humans instead of answered." | Workflow logic |
| 0:55-1:35 | Run sample BPA or engraving enquiry, then Approvals tab | "Here is an answerable ticket. The system identifies the persona, retrieves product or rate-card evidence, shows the trace, and prepares a draft. It still does not send anything. The draft waits in Approvals where the team can approve, edit, or reject it. This is a support copilot with human control, not an auto-send chatbot." | Working demo and safeguards |
| 1:35-2:10 | Run sustainability or carbon-neutral enquiry, CS Queue, KB draft | "Now a gap case. Carbon-neutral shipping and similar claims are not supported by the current sources, so the system blocks the answer and creates a CS queue item. After a human adds a verified resolution, it can draft an FAQ entry. That KB draft also needs review before it becomes an approved addition." | Gap loop and no hallucination |
| 2:10-2:45 | Marketing Intel tab | "The same support data becomes business intelligence. Across 70 tickets, the system detects nine themes and six marketing opportunities. It shows what customers ask that product pages should answer better, which personas care, and which themes are campaign-ready versus blocked by missing proof." | Marketing/product impact |
| 2:45-3:15 | Quality scorecard and README/SUBMISSION | "The measured run processed 70 tickets, queued 44 customer drafts, blocked 10 knowledge-gap tickets from hallucination, achieved 95.71 percent answerability accuracy, and kept 100 percent evidence coverage for answerable tickets. The local demo has zero incremental cost. A small pilot is estimated at roughly 37 to 75 dollars per month before credits." | Measurable impact and cost |
| 3:15-3:30 | Repo files and reset button | "The repo includes the backend, frontend, dataset guide, PRD, submission notes, and this script. Tests pass without live GLM or FPT credentials; live GLM is a conditional smoke test. Use Reset demo to return the chat, approvals, CS queue, and KB drafts to a clean judging state." | Proof of execution |
